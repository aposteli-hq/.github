# Aposteli

Aposteli is building a social platform for the faith ecosystem. It is open to everyone, grounded in Christ-like values, and brings Scripture into the way people connect, create, and share.

Our aim is a social home for people of faith, and anyone curious, where Scripture is part of the experience rather than an afterthought. Not another Bible app, but a platform where everyday life and faith meet.

## Product

### Feed
The main timeline. People post thoughts, questions, and moments, attach a verse when it fits, and react, comment, and reshare. Faith is woven naturally into an ordinary social feed.

### Discover
Search and exploration powered by semantic search, which finds posts, people, and even verses by meaning rather than keywords alone. It helps people connect around what matters to them.

### Games
Quick daily Scripture challenges. Light, repeatable ways to stay in the Word and keep the community coming back each day.

### Daily Bread
A daily rhythm of Scripture: a Verse of the Day, a Psalm, and a Proverb, surfaced to help people begin each day grounded.

## Our Approach

Aposteli is built around a few principles:

- **Open to everyone.** A platform grounded in Christ-like values, welcoming to all, not a closed community.
- **Scripture as part of life.** The Word belongs in the way we connect and create, available when wanted and never forced.
- **Community first.** Faith is meant to be shared, so the experience is built around people, not just content.
- **Privacy by design.** Personal data is handled deliberately and securely.
- **Practical and human.** The platform should stay simple, warm, and easy to use.
